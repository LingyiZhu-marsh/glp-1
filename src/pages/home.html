Molecular Pathways and Applications of GLP-1: From Gut Hormone to Modern Medicine

Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Trulicity. These brand names are everywhere, and they all belong to one family of drugs built around a single hormone your gut produces after every meal. This site follows hormone pathways from a cell in your intestine to a pill or weekly injection used by millions of people every week. It explains how these drugs work, what they’re approved for, how they differ from each other, and the future of this field.

Where to start

  • New to the topic? At a Glance answers the most common questions with no background needed.
  • Want the specifics? The Science goes inside the cell: the hormone, its receptor, how the drugs copy it, and a flowchart of the pathways.
  • Want real-world applications? In Practice covers approved uses, side effects and safety concerns, and where research is headed.
