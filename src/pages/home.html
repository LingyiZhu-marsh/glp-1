Molecular Pathways and Applications of GLP-1: From Gut Hormone to Modern Medicine

This website uses the structural differences between GLP-1 and GLP-1 RAs and their molecular pathways to explain the resulting physical effects of GLP-1. From there, the physical effects are used to justify the application of them in the medical field. I hope it serves as an informative resource on this “wonder-drug” that has been widely advertised and promoted in today's society.

Where to start

  • New to the topic? At a Glance answers the most common questions with no background needed.
  • Want the specifics? The Science goes inside the cell: the hormone, its receptor, how the drugs copy it, and a flowchart of the pathways.
  • Want real-world applications? In Practice covers approved uses, side effects and safety concerns, and where research is headed.
