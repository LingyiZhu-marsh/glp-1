In Practice

Future Research

Although GLP-1 medicines began as a treatment for type 2 diabetes, the recent popularity and research have pushed the topic and industry to new heights. Now, its expanding in several new directions at once. The next generation of drugs have become more complex. Drugs have begun targeting multiple receptors: tirzepatide adds GIP, retatrutide adds glucagon on top of both, and a five-receptor drug is in development.[@uab-glp1-revolution-2026] At the same time, scientists are exploring why these drugs protect the heart, kidneys, liver and joints even when weight loss is small. Lower inflammation may be the common trend, which raises the idea of lower doses aimed at organ protection rather than weight loss.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025] Researchers are also testing GLP-1 drugs for addiction and brain diseases, and trying to explain why some people lose much more weight than others.[@morello-emerging-uses-2026][@alexander-treatment-effects-2026] Big questions remain, including whether the receptor stays responsive after years of constant stimulation, since widespread use is still recent.[@uab-glp1-revolution-2026] This page looks at where this research stands and what is still unknown.

Next-generation drugs

  • Retatrutide (GLP-1 + GIP + glucagon): about 28% weight loss at 80 weeks in phase 3, approaching surgical results. It is still investigational.[@uab-glp1-revolution-2026][@hcplive-retatrutide-2026]
  • A five-receptor agonist in development adds PPARs, the target of older diabetes drugs, aiming to get their benefits without their side effects.[@uab-glp1-revolution-2026]
  • More oral options and combinations are coming, so drugs will increasingly compete on tolerability and safety.[@jalleh-safety-2026][@fda-priority-voucher-2026]

Rethinking how the drugs work

The 2026 mouse finding that long-term semaglutide activates AgRP “hunger” neurons challenges the textbook model. If confirmed, it may explain why these drugs keep weight off better than older appetite suppressants and point to new drug targets.[@dalton-glp1-study-2026]

Benefits beyond weight loss

Heart, kidney, liver, joint and artery conditions share chronic inflammation. GLP-1 drugs lower inflammatory markers (CRP, TNF-α, IL-6) partly regardless of weight loss.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025] Three routes are proposed: direct receptor action on immune and blood-vessel cells, indirect effects through weight loss and blood sugar, brain signals that dampen inflammation body-wide.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]

If these pathways are mapped, lower doses might give organ protection with fewer side effects and lower cost.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]

Brain disorders: a lesson in evidence

Observational data looked promising:

  • in a large cohort, semaglutide users had lower Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s risk[@morello-emerging-uses-2026]
  • exenatide improved Parkinson’s symptoms for a year after stopping[@morello-emerging-uses-2026]

But when oral semaglutide was tested properly in about 3,800 people with early Alzheimer’s (EVOKE and EVOKE+), it did not slow decline, even though some biomarkers improved.[@alzheimer-europe-evoke-2025] Associations in health records don’t prove a drug works, and that lesson applies to every “off-label” claim on this page.

Addiction

GLP-1 receptors in the brain’s reward system may create a kind of “substance satiety.” Observational studies link semaglutide to about 40% fewer opioid overdoses and 50% fewer alcohol intoxications. A small trial reduced alcohol cravings.[@morello-emerging-uses-2026] Large randomized trials are under way.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]

Other conditions being studied

PCOS, COPD and asthma, psoriasis and rheumatoid arthritis, COVID-19 outcomes, and short bowel syndrome. Most evidence so far is observational or from small studies.[@morello-emerging-uses-2026]

Open questions for the Field

  • Who responds: why do some people lose much more weight than others? Researchers hope to predict response from genetics and biology.[@alexander-treatment-effects-2026][@uab-glp1-revolution-2026]
  • Long-term effects: does constant receptor stimulation cause adaptation over decades?[@uab-glp1-revolution-2026]
  • Treatment length: what is the best duration, and the right dose for non-weight goals?[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]
  • Better safety tools: validated symptom measures and pharmacovigilance that accounts for reporting bias.[@jalleh-safety-2026]
  • Pending results: the FOCUS eye trial (expected 2027) and long-term liver outcomes from ESSENCE (expected 2029).[@jalleh-safety-2026][@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]
