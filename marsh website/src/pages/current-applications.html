Type 2 diabetes

Every GLP-1 RA is approved, alongside diet and exercise, to improve blood sugar control in type 2 diabetes. Metformin is still first-line. GLP-1 RAs are added when:

  • metformin isn’t tolerated
  • blood sugar goals aren’t met
  • HbA1c is 1.5% or more above target[@latif-glp1-comparison-2026]

Among long-acting drugs, semaglutide gives the largest drop in HbA1c and the most weight loss.[@latif-glp1-comparison-2026] GLP-1 RAs are not FDA-approved for type 1 diabetes. Trials of liraglutide as an add-on showed modest HbA1c drops (about 0.4%) and about 5 kg of weight loss.[@latif-glp1-comparison-2026]

Obesity

Approved drugs include liraglutide, injectable semaglutide, tirzepatide, and two pills: oral semaglutide and orforglipron. Each is used with diet and physical activity in adults with obesity, or with overweight plus at least one weight-related condition.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025][@healio-wegovy-pill-2025][@fda-priority-voucher-2026]

Typical weight loss in trials:

  • semaglutide 2.4 mg: 16.0% vs 5.7% on placebo over 68 weeks (STEP 3)[@alexander-treatment-effects-2026]
  • tirzepatide: 15.0–20.9% vs 3.1%[@alexander-treatment-effects-2026]
  • oral semaglutide: 13.6% counting everyone, 16.6% when taken as directed[@healio-wegovy-pill-2025]
  • orforglipron: 12.4% at 72 weeks[@fda-priority-voucher-2026]

A 2026 meta-analysis of 64 trials found similar weight loss across age, race, ethnicity, starting BMI and HbA1c. The one exception was sex: women lost 10.9% vs 6.8% for men. That difference was borderline (P = .08) and could partly reflect women’s lower average body weight.[@alexander-treatment-effects-2026] Weight loss in real-world use is smaller than in trials.[@alexander-treatment-effects-2026]

Heart and blood vessels

  • SELECT: 17,604 people with overweight or obesity and existing heart disease, no diabetes required. Semaglutide cut major cardiovascular events by 20% (6.5% vs 8.0%), and the benefit didn’t depend on how much weight was lost.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]
  • Diabetes trials: of seven cardiovascular outcome trials, all showed the drugs were safe for the heart. Liraglutide, injectable semaglutide and dulaglutide significantly reduced events.[@latif-glp1-comparison-2026]
  • Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF): semaglutide trials (STEP-HFpEF) and a tirzepatide trial (SUMMIT) improved symptoms and exercise capacity. In SUMMIT, cardiovascular death or worsening heart failure dropped by 38%.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]

Kidneys

  • FLOW: in people with type 2 diabetes and kidney disease, semaglutide cut major kidney events by 24% (18.7% vs 23.2%). The trial was stopped early because the benefit was clear, and it led to FDA approval in January 2025.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]
  • Dulaglutide: it slowed the decline in kidney function in moderate-to-severe kidney disease and is labeled as safe down to an eGFR of 15.[@latif-glp1-comparison-2026][@morello-emerging-uses-2026]

Liver

Semaglutide 2.4 mg received accelerated approval in August 2025 for MASH with moderate-to-advanced scarring (not cirrhosis). In interim trial results, 63% on semaglutide had MASH resolve vs 34% on placebo.[@fda-mash-2025] Liver cells carry little GLP-1R, so the benefit likely comes from weight loss and indirect effects on metabolism and inflammation.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025] Full outcome results are expected around 2029.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]

Sleep apnea, joints and arteries

  • Sleep apnea (SURMOUNT-OSA): tirzepatide reduced breathing interruptions by about 25–29 events per hour, vs about 5 on placebo.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]
  • Knee osteoarthritis (STEP 9): semaglutide lowered pain scores by 41.7 points vs 27.5 on placebo.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]
  • Peripheral artery disease (STRIDE): semaglutide increased maximum walking distance. The improvement was only modestly linked to weight loss.[@gonzalez-rellan-benefits-2025]

Choosing between drugs

There is no single “best” GLP-1 drug. Choice depends on patient preference (weekly vs daily, pill vs injection); other conditions, such as heart or kidney disease; side effects, convenience and cost.[@latif-glp1-comparison-2026] People generally stick with once-weekly drugs better.[@latif-glp1-comparison-2026] Cost is a major barrier: brand-name prices can exceed $1,000 a month, generic semaglutide isn’t expected in the US until at least 2032, and tirzepatide patents run to 2036.[@williams-compounded-glp1-2026]
